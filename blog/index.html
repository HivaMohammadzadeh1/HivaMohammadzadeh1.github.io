The four architectures at a glance

BERT

Encoder-only

Bidirectional. Reads context both ways for understanding tasks.
GPT-2

Decoder-only

Autoregressive. Generates one token at a time behind a causal mask.
T5

Encoder-decoder

Text-to-text. Cross-attention bridges the two halves.
LLaMA

Efficient decoder

SwiGLU and rotary embeddings. Trained on more data, not more parameters.
